# The Grammar of the Broken Mind

### Where cognitive distortions, the seven deadly sins, and every religion's catalogue of vice turn out to be the same map

> **The ask, in his words.** *"Bring together every single overlap between every single cognitive distortion and the seven deadly sins and all of their parallels across each religion… every religion has parallels for the seven deadly sins, Shadrupu… We basically want to solve religion, psychology, and human consciousness. Fuck all the classifications. Everything is a gray area when it comes to psychology as well. OCD, ADHD, dyslexia: these are all just buckets and human constructs."*

> **Decode of the garbled nouns:** *"Shadrupu"* → **Ṣaḍripu / Arishadvarga**, the six enemies of Hindu ethics. *"The Quran flood"* → the canonical **ʿUthmanic muṣḥaf** (the standardized Quran). *"The first version of whatever Judaism uses"* → the **Tanakh** (Torah + Nevi'im + Ketuvim). *"Every single Veda"* → Ṛg, Sāma, Yajur, Atharva, and the Upaniṣads that close them. Plus the **Bhagavad Gītā**, the **Pali Canon**, and the **Gurū Granth Sāhib**. If any decode is wrong, one line fixes it and the dossier gets re-pointed.

## 0. Read this before anything else

**This is a synthesis, not a scripture-fetch.** The session's egress policy returned HTTP 403 for every primary-text host attempted — Sefaria, Quran.com, BibleGateway, Wisdomlib, sacred-texts, the Vatican site. **No agent read a single primary text in full this session.** Every verse quoted below comes from the model's trained knowledge of these texts, cross-checked against web-search snippets. For texts this old and this stable that is a far smaller risk than it would be for frontier science — but it is not zero. **The single most likely defect in this document is a wrong chapter-and-verse number**, not a wrong idea. Every load-bearing citation carries a confidence mark, and a dedicated verifier agent re-checked the ones the argument leans on. Where a reference is marked *verify against source*, treat it as a pointer, not a proof.

**One error is pre-corrected, because everyone makes it.** The "seven deadly sins" are **not a list that appears anywhere in the Bible.** They are a patristic-to-medieval systematization — Evagrius's eight thoughts, Cassian's transmission, Gregory the Great's reduction to seven around 590 CE. Proverbs 6:16–19 ("six things the Lord hates, seven that are an abomination") is a *different* list and gets misquoted as the source constantly. This dossier keeps scripture and later systematization on separate shelves throughout.

**On "solving" religion, psychology, and consciousness.** We are not going to. What this document actually does is narrower and more defensible: it shows that at least seven independent civilizations, with no shared vocabulary and centuries of no contact, converged on nearly the same short list of ways a mind turns against itself — and that twentieth-century cognitive science, starting over from scratch and refusing all the theology, rediscovered the same list and relabeled it. That convergence is the real finding. The claim that these are "the same thing" is argued in §4 and then attacked, hard, in §5. The honest verdict lives at the bottom, not the top.

**His last instruction is the method, not a mood.** *"Fuck all the classifications. Everything is a gray area."* That is not an aside — it is the thesis's spine. The wager of this whole document is that pride, mada, kibr, gaʾavah, ahaṅkāra, and "grandiosity/personalization" are not six things that resemble each other. They are one continuous structure in the mind that six cultures each drew a different border around. Same as OCD, ADHD, and dyslexia: real phenomena, arbitrary fences. The map is not the territory. This dossier is a catalogue of maps, laid over each other until the territory shows through the gaps.
