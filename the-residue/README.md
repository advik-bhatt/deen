# The Residue

### Strip ego, matter, sense, and emotion from every religion. What's actually left — by the texts, the measurements, and the brain scans

> **The ask, in his words.** *"Anything, ANYTHING materialistic written in religion is viewed by the culture. Culture is our religion. I'm talking about the Ego-free, material-free, sense-free, emotion-free truths. Unite them all. Classify everything else as bullshit. This is not a tiny task. Understand the scope of this and find research that supports this to serve as your source of truth and based on that go fucking crazy."*

> **The thesis, restated so it can be wrong:** *(a) religion's material/ethical/ritual content is culture doing social work; (b) each tradition, when it strips ego, matter, sense, and emotion, converges on (c) one identical universal core; and (d) the cultural layer is noise that can be classified away.* Four claims. The evidence sorts them cleanly, and not all the same way.

What follows is the research that *bears on* it — and much of it genuinely supports him, more than the average educated skeptic would guess. But a source of truth assembled only from agreeing sources is a mirror, not a source. The disagreeing evidence is in here, load-bearing, and the verdict below is what survives both directions.

**The verdict, up front, in one paragraph.** Claim (a) has to be split before it can be scored, because it is two claims wearing one sentence. (a1): *the outer layer does measurable social work* — cooperation at scale, commitment signaling, boundary maintenance. That is well-supported; it is close to the founding consensus of the social-scientific study of religion (§1). (a2): *therefore the layer is only culture, and its commands have no other source.* That is not a finding — it is the methodological assumption the field starts from (a social science can only see the social), promoted into a conclusion. This dossier endorses (a1) and refuses (a2): a law revealed by the One who made the creature would be *expected* to do social work — "Does He who created not know?" (Q 67:14) — so the functionalist data cannot distinguish "culture invented it" from "revelation, doing what it was sent to do." Claim (b) is real in a specific, bounded, measurable way that is the live edge of current research. Claim (c) — *one identical universal core* — is **not supported and on the best current reading refuted**: the traditions that ran the stripping protocol most rigorously contradicted each other about what remains, for fifteen centuries, on the record. Claim (d) — *classify the rest as bullshit* — is **refuted by the traditions themselves**, who unanimously treat the outer layer as the non-optional vehicle and police "kick the ladder early" as their signature internal heresy. Net: **strong thesis dead, weak thesis live** — and the weak thesis is still a big, mostly-unfinished research program with his name on the right side of two of its four claims.

## 1. What the social science actually shows — and the reductive step it smuggles

The *functional* half of the claim is not a hot take. It is roughly what the social-scientific study of religion has said since 1912. But read this section knowing what the method is: every author below practices methodological naturalism — the discipline's ground rule is that only social and cognitive causes are admissible — so "the social causes are the only causes" is the entry ticket, never a result. What follows is their account, reported as theirs.

**Durkheim** (*Les Formes élémentaires de la vie religieuse*, 1912) claims the sacred is society worshipping itself — the totem is the clan's flag, and the god is the group experienced as a force; on his account the material and moral content of religion *is* culture, by construction. (That last move is the (a2) assumption from §0 stated at maximum strength — an atheological premise doing its work in the open, not an empirical finding.) **Mary Douglas** (*Purity and Danger*, 1966, ch. 3 on the abominations of Leviticus): the food laws are social classification and boundary-maintenance — the dietary rules are culture doing structural work, which is exactly his "anything materialistic written in religion is viewed by the culture." **Norenzayan** (*Big Gods*, 2013): moralizing, punishing, all-seeing gods appear as societies scale past face-to-face monitoring — the *ethics* layer is cooperation technology. **Sosis & Bressler**: 19th-century religious communes with costlier ritual requirements outlasted secular ones — ritual as commitment signaling. **Boyer and Atran**: religious concepts are minimally counterintuitive products of ordinary cognition — the supernatural content rides on standard mental hardware. *(Honest wrinkle in this literature, reported because it's the method: the Whitehouse et al. 2019 Nature paper claiming complex societies precede moralizing gods was retracted in 2021 over a missing-data artifact — the big-gods-and-complexity ordering is still contested. The retraction is confirmed: Nature 568:226–229; Retraction Note 10.1038/s41586-021-03656-3.)*

So: the festival, the food rule, the funeral rite, the modesty code, the tithe — the evidence says this layer *does* track social scale, cooperation problems, and boundary defense. What the evidence cannot say is that social work is all the layer is, because a functional benefit is exactly what a true revelation would also produce — the law of the One who created would fit the creature (Q 67:14), and observing the fit proves the fit, not the absence of the Lawgiver. The dietary law, for one, announces its own source in plain terms (Q 5:3), and mistaking inherited custom for revelation is the Quran's *own* named error (Q 2:170) — the two categories are distinguished inside the text more sharply than Durkheim distinguishes them. Where the same literature bites back on its own terms is §5.

## 2. Where he's right, part two: the traditions made his distinction themselves

The strongest textual support for the two-layer model is that **the traditions independently invented it.** This is not a modern imposition; it's native doctrine, with canon behind it:

- **Vedanta:** Mundaka Upaniṣad 1.1.4–5 **[H]** — scripture itself files the *entire Vedic ritual corpus*, all four Vedas and their sciences, under *aparā vidyā* ("lower knowledge"), reserving *parā vidyā* for attainment of the Imperishable. This is the single strongest locus in the whole dossier: the canon classifying its own outer layer as the lesser knowledge. Śaṅkara systematizes it as *vyāvahārika* (conventional) vs *pāramārthika* (ultimate) reality, and *karma-kāṇḍa* (ritual portion) vs *jñāna-kāṇḍa* (knowledge portion).
- **Sufism:** the *sharīʿah → ṭarīqah → ḥaqīqah* ladder — law as vehicle, path as method, truth as destination. Rumi's Masnavi Book V preface **[H]**: religion as candle, journey, arrival — the light is for the road, not the road for the light.
- **Kabbalah:** *PaRDeS* — four reading depths ending in *sod* (secret); Zohar III:152a **[H]** — the Torah's narratives are its *garment*, and "woe to those who take the garment for the Torah itself."
- **Buddhism:** the two truths (Nāgārjuna, MMK 24.8–10 **[H]**) and the raft simile (MN 22 **[H]**) — the teaching is a raft for crossing, "not for holding onto."
- **Christianity:** "the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life" (2 Cor 3:6 **[H]**); the paired cataphatic/apophatic tracks; Eckhart's God (as conceived) vs *Gottheit* (beyond conception).
- **North Indian bhakti:** the *sagun/nirgun* split, with Kabir and Nanak explicitly on the formless side.

He is in old, distinguished company. **Schuon stated his exact thesis in 1948** (*The Transcendent Unity of Religions*: exoteric difference, esoteric unity), Huxley in 1945, and the Upaniṣads arguably in the first millennium BCE. That cuts both ways — see §6.

## 3. The residue itself: what the stripping actually finds

This is the positive result, and it's real. Three independent kinds of evidence converge on it.

### 3.1 The texts converge on an *operation* — a four-fold negation

Across lineages, the apophatic corpus executes a structurally near-identical move: **deny sense-access → deny conceptual predication → deny both members of every binary pair → deny the denial itself.**

- Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad 7 **[H]** on *turīya*, the "fourth" state: *"not inwardly cognitive, not outwardly cognitive, not both, not a cognition-mass, not cognitive, not non-cognitive; unseen, ungraspable, without characteristics, unthinkable, indescribable."*
- Kena 1.3 **[H]**: *"the eye does not go there, nor speech, nor mind."*
- Bṛhadāraṇyaka 2.3.6 / 4.5.15 **[H]**: *neti neti* — "not this, not this."
- Nāgārjuna's tetralemma **[H]**: not existent, not non-existent, not both, not neither.
- Pseudo-Dionysius, *Mystical Theology* ch. 5 **[H]**: the divine is "beyond assertion and denial."
- Daodejing 1 and 14 **[H]**: "the dao that can be spoken is not the eternal dao"; "looked at but not seen, listened to but not heard."
- Ein Sof / *ayin* in Kabbalah; Maimonides' negative attributes (*Guide* I.58 **[H]**: only negations are true of God); the Quran's *"everything perishes except His face"* (Q 28:88, 55:26–27 **[H]**); Eckhart's Sermon 52 **[H]** ("I pray God to rid me of God"); the Mul Mantar's *akāl mūrat, ajūnī* (timeless, unborn) **[H]**.
- And the convergence was noticed *from inside*, centuries ago: **Azriel of Gerona** (13th c.) remarks that comprehension of the ultimate "is solely by means of *no*." Michael Sells (*Mystical Languages of Unsaying*, 1994) documents the formal convergence rigorously.

*Red-team correction folded in:* the first-pass claim "in lineages with no plausible contact" was too strong. Azriel sits downstream of Neoplatonism — the same stream as Dionysius — and Greek-Indian contact is a live question, not a settled negative. And the corpus is *selected*: each tradition's apophatic strand is a minority report (the bulk of the Vedas is cataphatic ritual; most Christianity is affirmative theology; most Buddhism is devotional). The defensible claim: **a recurrent apophatic grammar, partly independent (the ancient instances — Māṇḍūkya, Nāgārjuna, Dionysius — predate any modern perennialist instrument), targeting exactly the layers he named: sense, concept, and the bounded self.**

### 3.2 The measurements converge on a *state*

- **Hood's Mysticism Scale** (operationalizing Stace's 1960 "introvertive core"): the same three-factor structure confirmed across **US Christians and Iranian Muslims** (Hood, Ghorbani, Watson et al. 2001, *JSSR* 40:691–705, samples N=1,379/188 US, 185 Iran — verified) and **Chinese Buddhist monastics** (Chen, Hood et al. 2011).
- **MEQ30** (MacLean, Leoutsakos, Johnson & Griffiths 2012 — 1,602 respondents, four factors, verified; Barrett et al. 2015): unity, noetic quality, ineffability, positive mood — replicating across psilocybin studies.
- **Ego-Dissolution Inventory** (Nour et al. 2016 — α=.93 verified; the reported correlation with the unitive core, ρ≈.735, could not be re-verified this session — treat as pointer).
- **Metzinger's MPE project** (*Minimal Phenomenal Experience*, Phil. & the Mind Sciences 1(I), 2020, DOI 10.33735/phimisci.2020.I.46; *The Elephant and the Blind*, MIT Press 2024): 1,403 usable respondents from a 3,627-person meditator survey — a coherent, structured "pure awareness" report class: wakeful, contentless, selfless, non-dual. **Crucially, this instrument was *not* built from Stace's template and still found the report class** — the best answer so far to the circularity problem. *(Precision the red team enforced: the survey ran in five European languages among predominantly Western meditators — it is not yet a cross-tradition result — and it found twelve factors, not one.)*
- Griffiths' God-encounter survey (2019): drug-occasioned and non-drug experiences are *similar* on core phenomenology — but the paper also reports real group differences, so "indistinguishable except the label" overstates it.

**What the state converges on is an absence-structure:** no bounded self, no sense-object, no discursive concept, no subject/object partition, no time — wakeful awareness with the self-model suspended. That is *his* list — ego-free, material-free, sense-free — as measured phenomenology.

### 3.3 The brain converges on a *capacity* — and diverges on implementation

- The **self is a model, not a thing** (Metzinger, *Being No One*, 2003) — and the brain can stop running it. Predictive-processing accounts make this mechanistic (Letheby & Gerrans 2017, "self unbound"; Laukkonen & Slagter 2021, "from many to (n)one").
- Multiple unrelated routes in — concentrative meditation (Brewer 2011, *PNAS* 108(50):20254–59, DMN deactivation in experienced meditators — verified), psilocybin/LSD (Carhart-Harris 2012, *PNAS* 109(6):2138–43 — verified; REBUS 2019), jhāna at 7T and cessation EEG (Sacchet's Harvard/MGH program; Chowdhury et al. 2023) — all suspend the self-model and produce the same *reported* phenomenology.
- **But the measured signatures diverge:** meditation *quiets* the DMN; LSD *raises* global connectivity. Same report, different implementation — fatal to "one identical brain state," compatible with "one capacity, many routes." And even a shared signature would show a shared *brain state*, never a shared *metaphysical referent*. That category line is bright and stays bright.

## 4. Where the strong thesis dies: the residue won't agree on itself

The decisive test case was run in history, at full rigor, for ~1,500 years: **Vedānta vs Buddhism.**

Both ran the complete stripping protocol. Both produced virtuoso negation literature. And they contradicted each other about what remains: Vedānta's fully negated residue is a *real, permanent Self* — *neti neti* is immediately followed by "its name is the Truth of truth" (Bṛh. 2.3.6), and *nirguṇa* Brahman is positively glossed *sat-chit-ānanda*. Buddhism's fully negated result is *anattā/śūnyatā* — the denial of exactly that Self. Śaṅkara wrote polemics against the Buddhist schools (Brahmasūtra-bhāṣya II.2); the Mādhyamikas returned fire. Either the negations underdetermine the metaphysics — in which case textual convergence proves *grammar*, not *core* — or one tradition misdescribed its own summit, in which case texts cannot settle the question at all.

Worse for the strong form, **the most rigorous apophaticist in the corpus explicitly forbids the thesis's final step.** Nāgārjuna: those who convert emptiness into a view are "incurable" (MMK 13.8 **[H]**, verified; reading it as anti-residue is interpretation, but the anti-reification point is verbatim), and there is "not the slightest difference" between saṃsāra and nirvāṇa (MMK 25.19–20 **[H]**) — no hidden signal-layer behind the conventional world. The Heart Sutra negates precisely the categories Vedānta retains (no consciousness, no attainment).

And the **"strip emotion" clause specifically fails** on the Christian and Sufi material: *The Cloud of Unknowing* strips knowledge and images but keeps *love* as the one faculty that reaches God — "smite upon that thick cloud of unknowing with a sharp dart of longing love." John of the Cross's *nada* is ascetical means to *loving union* with a personal God, not an attributeless absolute. Junayd's sober school insists *fanāʾ* is not terminal — *baqāʾ* returns the mystic to subsistence and servanthood, self restored with divinely bestowed attributes. **Several traditions deliberately un-strip after stripping.** Three architectures, not one: substrate-apophasis (something real remains and gets positively glossed), therapeutic non-residue apophasis (the expectation of a residue is itself the disease), and semantic-regulative apophasis (Maimonides: a discipline on *language*, explicitly not a contemplative's report).

One more crack at the base, flagged because the synthesis initially under-flagged it: **the reportability problem.** Metzinger and Windt treat it as *open* whether a genuinely contentless, selfless state can be encoded, remembered, and reported at all — the standing worry is retrospective confabulation, or covert content that makes the state not contentless after all. The entire residue rests on testimony whose possibility-conditions are contested. Not fatal; not dismissable.

## 5. Where the disposal claim dies: the noise is load-bearing

"Classify everything else as bullshit" fails twice, on the traditions' own terms and on the evidence's.

**The traditions police exactly this move as their signature heresy.** Every two-level tradition binds the levels: Nāgārjuna's own two-truths locus says the ultimate *cannot be taught* without foundation in the conventional (MMK 24.10); Junayd binds *ḥaqīqah* to *sharīʿah*; the raft is gripped until the far shore; Śaṅkara requires *karma-kāṇḍa* purification (*citta-śuddhi*) as the qualification for *jñāna*; Lurianic Kabbalah makes ritual *more* binding, not less (every commandment a cosmic repair); Dōgen collapses the distinction entirely (*shushō-ittō* — practice IS realization; chop wood, carry water). And the historical experiment was run: **Sabbatianism** — the 17th-century movement that concluded the inner truth abolishes the outer law — is the paradigm case Scholem documented ("Redemption Through Sin"), and its own community anathematized it. The move he's proposing has a name in every tradition, and the name is *the characteristic failure mode*.

**And the "noise" is what religion mostly is and does.** The lived-religion scholarship (McGuire, Orsi, Ammerman) is blunt: religion as practiced by nearly everyone who has ever practiced it *is* the embodied, material, communal layer. The contemplative elite running the stripping protocol was always a tiny minority, often viewed with suspicion by its own tradition. The cultural layer does measurable work — cooperation at scale, commitment signaling, boundary maintenance, death-anxiety management (terror management, Vail et al.), solidarity (Durkheim's collective effervescence). §1's own evidence — the part that proves "culture is our religion" — is the same evidence that proves the culture-layer isn't noise. **He has signal and noise reversed for the population-level phenomenon.** The defensible version of his stance is the one the traditions themselves supply: *keep the forms and aim through them* — a practice orientation, not a demolition order. The Quran's own architecture says the same thing from the other direction: the religion is declared *perfected* with its law intact (Q 5:3), and a decided matter is not left optional for the believer (Q 33:36) — the outer layer is not scaffolding that the inner state licenses anyone to kick away.

## 6. The trap he has to answer, and the best answer available

**At full strength:** the stripping operation is not a view from nowhere. It is performed from inside a culture — specifically, a datable one. Schuon's Traditionalism (his thesis, 1948) was born in French occultist milieux and carries a documented politics (Guénon→Evola→Dugin; Sedgwick, *Against the Modern World* — confirmed). Asad shows the very category "religion-as-private-experience-separable-from-practice" is a modern Western, post-Reformation, colonial-era construction. Sharf shows "the real core of Buddhism is meditative experience" is *Buddhist modernism* — a 19th–20th-century reform product. Katz supplies the epistemology: *"There are NO pure (i.e., unmediated) experiences"* — the tradition constitutes the experience, not just the report. And the empirical teeth: the flagship instruments (M-Scale, MEQ) were built from Stace's criteria, and Stace built his typology in dialogue with transnational Vedanta (Breau & Gillis-Smith 2023 — confirmed) — so the strip-and-find machine keeps "finding" a Vedantic-Protestant template it was built from. Chen's Chinese data even shows a *structural divergence* (the two unity factors merged) that the authors reported as support — confirmation bias on the record. **The thesis needs the residue exempt from the acid it pours on everything else, and no principled exemption has survived.**

**The best answer concedes the trap and keeps what survives it.** Four moves: (1) *Forman's crack:* a genuinely contentless event has no content for culture to construct — mediation bites on interpretation and report, not obviously on bare occurrence. It defends a thin blank, not a rich identical core. (2) *The surprised mystics:* Eckhart and Böhme had experiences their doctrines forbade; if concepts fully determined experience, heresy-by-experience would be impossible. Hard constructivism is false; **soft** constructivism — shaped, never exhaustively constructed — is where the field actually sits. (3) *Genealogy is not refutation:* showing perennialism has an address kills its neutrality, not its referent — and the four-fold negation is ancient, pre-dating the colonial instrument by a millennium-plus. (4) *Downgrade and survive:* what the evidence supports is not "one identical culture-free universal signal" but **a shaped, cross-culturally recurrent experiential attractor whose invariant is an absence-structure, carried by common cognitive and neural hardware.** That claim never asserts culture-freedom — and it stays silent on the referent, which is where the neuroscience's writ ends anyway (§3.3's own bright line: a shared brain state is never evidence for or against what the state is *of*). So the trap has nothing to grip.

That downgraded claim is the *live* thesis. It is weaker than what he asked for and much stronger than nothing: a reproducible mode of human awareness, reachable by unrelated routes, with the self-model suspended, described by the same negations on four continents across three millennia.

## 7. What would actually settle it

The field has run almost none of the decisive experiments. Four designs, in rising cost:

1. **The cheap falsifier (runnable now, on existing data):** take the contentless-awareness reports already collected (the MPE corpus), strip the tradition labels, and have blind raters classify each report back to its tradition using only the core phenomenology. Above-chance classification → "culture-free residue" fails hard. At-chance → it survives its most direct test. Nearly free.
2. **The circularity-breaker:** cross-cultural phenomenology with instruments *not* derived from Stace — emic item generation inside each tradition, pre-registered, analyzed blind. If a Stace-shaped core appears anyway, the common core gains real footing. MPE-92M is a partial start (non-Stace items, still found the class — but twelve factors, and a Western sample).
3. **The Katz-vs-Forman adjudicator:** matched induction (same psychedelic dose or same protocol), practitioners from different traditions, content coded blind. Strong prediction-from-tradition → constructivism; weak → core. The single most decisive design, essentially never run.
4. **The moonshot:** a neural signature of pure awareness invariant across both routes (chemical, meditative) *and* across traditions — converting Forman's Pure Consciousness Event from philosophical stipulation into biomarker. Current data says the signatures diverge; finding an invariant deep-marker would be the biggest result in the field.

The disposal half (claim d) can't be settled by experiment because it's historical — and the record already answered it (§5). That half is closed.

## 8. The three tiers

**Tier 1 — now.** The subtractive technique is real, documented, and teachable; the state it targets is reproducibly inducible by contemplative practice, and the instruments to measure it exist (M-Scale, MEQ30, EDI). The psychedelic literature stays in this dossier as *data about brains* — it is evidence in §3, and that is all it is here: intoxicants are categorically closed as a practice by the Quran (Q 5:90–91), so the chemical route is other people's experiment to run, never his door in. The stance that is his to adopt today is the one §5 already forced: *keep the forms and aim through them* — the ladder Rumi, Śaṅkara, and Nāgārjuna all police is climbed, not discarded. What cannot be claimed at Tier 1: that the state delivers one identical universal metaphysical signal. The honest usable claim: **a cross-culturally accessible mode of awareness with the self-model switched off.**

**Tier 2 — 6–24 months, buildable.** Run designs 1–3 above. None needs new physics — money, pre-registration, cross-tradition recruitment. Design 1 is nearly free and directly attacks the load-bearing claim; a well-scoped version could be proposed to the Metzinger MPE group or Yaden's Hopkins lab this quarter (drafted outreach in [`OUTREACH.md`](OUTREACH.md)).

**Tier 3 — the moonshot.** The invariant neural signature of the residue (design 4). Cost is real (7T scanner time, adept recruitment across traditions — Sacchet's program is the existing infrastructure); the cheapest falsifying experiment is design 1 and costs approximately nothing.
